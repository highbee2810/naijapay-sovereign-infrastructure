# NDPC Cross-Border Data Transfer Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary explains key concepts used in the Nigerian data protection framework governing cross-border transfers of personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and guidance issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

---

# A

## Accountability

The obligation of a Data Controller or Data Processor to demonstrate compliance with applicable data protection requirements. Accountability requires organizations to maintain records, implement safeguards, assess risks, and provide evidence of compliance during regulatory reviews or audits.

### Example
A company maintains documented transfer impact assessments and contractual safeguards for all international data transfers.

---

## Adequacy Decision

A formal determination that a foreign country, territory, sector, or international organization provides a level of personal data protection equivalent or substantially comparable to that required under Nigerian data protection law.

Where an adequacy decision exists, personal data may generally be transferred to that jurisdiction
